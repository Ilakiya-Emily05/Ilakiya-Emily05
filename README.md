## About Me

I’m a pre-final year Computer Science Engineering student focused on building applied AI systems, scalable backend architectures, and production-oriented software.

My work sits at the intersection of artificial intelligence, computer vision, backend engineering, and performance-aware systems design.

I enjoy building systems that are not just technically interesting, but actually useful under real-world constraints.

From GPU-aware AI orchestration to ethical AI systems and climate-focused technology, I care about engineering that scales intelligently and solves meaningful problems.

Outside engineering, I write poetry and paint, which probably explains both the systems thinking and occasional aesthetic obsession.
